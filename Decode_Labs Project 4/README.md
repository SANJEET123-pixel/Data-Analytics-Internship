# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## About the Project

This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset using **Power BI** to understand sales, customers, orders, products, payments, coupons, and referral sources.

The dashboard contains **3 pages** with different types of analysis.

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – E-Commerce Sales Analytics

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity
* Unique Customers
* Average Order Value
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Sales by Product
* Order Status
* Payment Methods

### Page 2 – Customer & Behavior Analysis

* Customer analysis
* Top Customers by Sales
* Referral Source Sales
* Cart Items vs Order Value
* Coupon Usage

### Page 3 – Product & Business Insights

* Total Products
* Total Quantity
* Total Sales
* Average Unit Price
* Average Order Value
* Quantity Sold by Product
* Average Unit Price by Product
* Product Price vs Total Sales
* Product Sales Share

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX

## Dataset

The dataset contains **1,200 e-commerce records** with information related to orders, customers, products, pricing, payments, order status, coupons, referrals, and other order details.

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to understand e-commerce sales and customer behavior through interactive dashboards and identify useful business insights from the data.
